How the grade is calculated

Course grade = 60% × adjusted regular exam average + 20% × final exam + 20% × homework average.

The five regular exams count equally. When replacement is enabled, a higher final exam score replaces exactly one lowest regular exam score in the exam average. The final exam still counts for its own 20% category. The replacement never lowers the grade.

Assumed letter-grade cutoffs: A ≥ 90%, B ≥ 80%, C ≥ 70%, D ≥ 60%. A displayed score needed is rounded up to two decimals. Calculations use unrounded values.
